# GlassPlay visual system

## Direction
An independent entertainment collection, presented with the pacing of a film programme: one featured encounter, strong typography, generous dark space and a deliberate transition into each experience. The first viewport makes the smart-glasses category and the next action visible together. The explicit brief governs this direction; the initial mint brand and Nevermore's spectral palette are retained and expanded.

## Language
Manrope for navigation and readable supporting copy. Newsreader for expressive display typography. Self-hosted Latin variable fonts, three WOFF2 files, OFL notices. No remote font requests. Off-white on green-black for GlassPlay; lavender-black for Nevermore. The mint accent belongs to GlassPlay, not every seasonal experience.

## Composition
Homepage: tagline and an arched featured poster; a film invitation; three labelled seasonal concepts; a concise About section. Nevermore: large title, cinematic artwork, silence/presence narrative, actual film or an intentional release-held title state, three encounters, four interaction steps, compatibility and expandable practical details. No fake play/download buttons. Credits/privacy is a quiet reading surface.

## Media states
The default build uses original title artwork and concept graphics. The local review uses approved runtime-derived artwork and labelled Simulator footage, staged outside public/. Publication requires separate explicit policy and exact file hashes. Production artwork is never represented as a photograph of physical Prism. Gallery scenes have text alternatives; the film has captions and a transcript. Playback is intentional, never autoplay with sound.

## Interaction and motion
Native links, native video controls, semantic details/summary. Visible two-pixel focus and skip link. One restrained featured-poster hover; reduced-motion removes it and smooth scrolling. No entrance animation hides content. Mobile navigation stays visible without a menu dependency. Private review uses noindex; the deployable fallback has production canonicals and metadata.

## Responsive behaviour
1328px content cap. Desktop paired compositions collapse to a deliberately ordered mobile sequence at760px. Small seasonal concepts become horizontal editorial rows. Body text remains readable and paragraphs are bounded. All imagery reserves intrinsic space; no layout depends on client JavaScript.

## Review benchmark
Annapurna Interactive's image-led selection and Playdead's restraint inform the content hierarchy, not a copied visual style. GlassPlay needs a warmer, broader seasonal identity and clearer availability/device distinction. Sources inspected2026-10-21: https://annapurnainteractive.com/ and https://playdead.com/.
