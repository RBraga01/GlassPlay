# GlassPlay
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Astro, TypeScript, modern CSS, GitHub Actions and GitHub Pages, explicitly selected by the owner's implementation brief (21 October 2026).

## Users
People discovering seasonal interactive experiences for AR smart glasses. The first experience is Nevermore; Windows and Raven Simulator preview evidence exists. Physical Prism remains unverified.

## Product Purpose
A small, memorable destination where visitors understand an experience, see authentic demonstrations, check device validation and obtain a package when distribution is authorized.

## Positioning
An independent collection of authored encounters that happen in the wearer's field of view. Not a general XR store, SDK or operating system.

## Capabilities and Constraints
English content. Static website, no accounts, telemetry, backend or shared game engine. Nevermore remains the application source of truth. Curated source preview, private release candidate and public website are separate. Private video and original models cannot be published by implication. Code/art licensing, SDK permission, public launch and private-repository Pages eligibility require owner decisions. No commercial availability or physical-device claims.

## Brand Commitments
GlassPlay. “A little magic, right before your eyes.” Category: Interactive Experiences for AR Smart Glasses. Dark refined presentation with restrained motion. GlassPlay is season-neutral; Nevermore retains its own Halloween atmosphere and approved art. Christmas, Easter and St. Patrick's Day remain concepts. The owner’s original navy/violet AR-glasses identity is the collection authority. PR #1 rejects the subsequent mint/arch direction. New professional rendered artwork must communicate smart glasses immediately; Nevermore retains a separate Halloween world.

## Evidence on Hand
The explicit implementation brief supplies product and design direction and authorizes autonomous routine choices. Twenty authored Nevermore events, approved production renders, actual Windows/Room recordings, exact source manifests and a private RC1 package. Real media is prepared locally pending publication approval. No testimonials, hardware footage or public download permission supplied.

## Product Principles
- Show an experience before explaining its implementation.
- Keep the catalogue the single discovery authority.
- Treat availability and device validation separately.
- Ship honest, intentional states rather than invented downloads.
- No publication without explicit owner authorization.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, explicit media controls, captions/text alternatives, responsive mobile layouts and measured Lighthouse targets.
